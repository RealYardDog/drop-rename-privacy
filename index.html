Drop Rename Privacy Policy

Drop Rename is a Mac app by Eric Yardley Digital LLC.

The app reads files you choose and suggests new names on your own Mac. It does not collect, store, or send personal data to Eric Yardley Digital LLC.

There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Suggested names are made on your Mac with Apple’s on-device intelligence, Vision, and PDFKit. Your files are not uploaded.

The free rename count, your naming settings, and rename history stay on this Mac.

Purchases on the Mac App Store are handled by Apple. Apple’s privacy policy is at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.
